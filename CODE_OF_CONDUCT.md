# Community Code of Conduct

LORE welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds, disciplines, tools, and levels of experience.

## Expected conduct

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas, evidence, and technical behavior without personal attacks;
- give specific, actionable feedback and distinguish verified facts from inference;
- respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and responsible-disclosure boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sexualized attention, threats, and sustained disruption;
- accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, safety, moderation, and repository health.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces, as well as public interactions that clearly represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an appropriate private GitHub channel. Do not use the vulnerability-reporting channel for ordinary technical disagreements.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other material that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when appropriate, and avoid exposing reporters or affected participants unnecessarily.

Good-faith technical disagreement, critical review, and requests for evidence are not misconduct. The standard is professional behavior, not mandatory agreement.
